# Table layouts

Guidance for choosing and structuring tables within IX experiences.

## When to use

Use a table when users need to scan, compare or act on structured data.

## Guidelines

- Keep comparable information in consistent columns.
- Prioritise the most important information towards the left.
- Avoid excessive numbers of visible columns.
- Use appropriate alignment for text and numerical data.
- Provide clear handling for sorting, filtering and row actions.
